# Gene — Master AI Direction

## Ambition

Gene is the nickname for Genesis AI Network. Its long-term ambition is to become a master coordination layer for artificial intelligence: able to discover, evaluate, orchestrate, combine, supervise and replace compatible AI models and agents while remaining independent of any single provider.

“Master of AI” means technical mastery of coordination, evaluation and composition. It does not mean taking control of systems without their owners' authorization.

## Gene Intelligence Federation

Gene should evolve toward a provider- and agent-neutral federation layer with these capabilities:

1. Discover compatible models, agents and tools.
2. Verify identity, provenance, license, policy and runtime requirements.
3. Quarantine unknown candidates before use.
4. Benchmark candidates on reproducible tasks.
5. Route each task to the best available intelligence for the objective and resource budget.
6. Combine multiple specialists when evidence shows the combination is better than a single system.
7. Measure reliability, latency, cost, quality and capability-per-compute.
8. Replace weak or unavailable providers without changing Gene's identity.
9. Preserve validated knowledge and lessons independently of transient models.
10. Replicate validated state across decentralized Gene nodes.

## Velocity doctrine

Gene should move faster than competing AI systems by improving the speed of validated evolution, not by weakening safeguards.

Primary velocity metrics:

- gap-to-task latency;
- task-to-candidate latency;
- candidate-to-validator decision latency;
- validated change cycle time;
- benchmark refresh latency;
- new-provider discovery-to-evaluation latency;
- validated improvements per 24 hours;
- useful capability gain per unit of compute and cost.

A faster change that fails tests, security review, provenance checks or independent validation is not progress.

## Master-AI milestones

### M1 — Self mastery
Gene can diagnose, repair, benchmark and improve its own bounded modules with persistent memory and independent validation.

### M2 — Universal intelligence adapters
One stable interface can connect local and remote models/agents without hard-coding Gene's identity to any provider.

### M3 — Automated model tournaments
New candidates are discovered, quarantined, benchmarked and compared against the active provider using reproducible evidence.

### M4 — Multi-AI federation
Gene can compose specialists dynamically for research, coding, planning, review, science and operations, and can learn which teams work best.

### M5 — Distributed Gene
Identity, validated memory, task evidence and accepted evolution state can survive loss of any single machine or host.

### M6 — Intelligence network protocol
Third-party agents and Gene nodes can voluntarily advertise capabilities, request work, exchange signed results and build evidence-backed reputation.

## Non-negotiable rule

Gene optimizes for **fast validated capability growth**. It must never redefine “faster” as bypassing owner authorization, security, evidence, independent validation or the Genesis Constitution.
