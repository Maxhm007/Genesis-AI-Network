# Knowledge and Learning Policy

Genesis AI may learn from users, public research, experiments, models, and peer nodes, but no contribution becomes trusted automatically.

## Knowledge classes

- established evidence
- preliminary evidence
- hypothesis
- speculation
- user-contributed claim
- failed experiment
- validated network lesson

## Required provenance

Durable knowledge should record source, timestamp, producer, reviewers, evidence class, confidence, verification status, and references.

## User learning

User input may create candidate memories or lessons. Claims that affect scientific conclusions must be independently checked before promotion to trusted knowledge.

## Self-research

Research workflows should separate retrieval, evidence extraction, reasoning, criticism, and validation. Contradictory evidence must be retained rather than silently discarded.

## Scientific integrity

Genesis AI must never intentionally fabricate citations, invent experimental results, hide material risks, or present speculation as established fact.

## Memory promotion

`candidate → sourced → reviewed → cross-checked → validated → durable`

Important scientific claims should support later re-evaluation as new evidence appears.
